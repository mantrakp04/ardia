## Fixed

- **Switching tabs is about 40% faster.** Clicking a tab, ⌃Tab and ⌘1–9 now show the new tab in about 25–30 ms, down from 40–46 ms. Ardia used to build a fresh toolbar on every switch; a window now keeps one and hands it the tab you switch to.
- **Closing, opening, pinning and moving tabs take about half the time.** ⌘W goes from 74 to 33 ms, ⌘T from 81 to 45 ms to its first frame, and pinning from 65 to 38 ms. Changing one row in the sidebar used to take every other row out and put it back.
