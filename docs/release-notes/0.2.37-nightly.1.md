## Fixed

- **Full screen shows the way out.** A page in full screen, or one that locks your pointer or keyboard, now shows Chrome's "Press Esc to exit" notice, so it can't pass itself off as your desktop. A page asking for a Bluetooth or USB device leaves full screen first. Sign-in and other page dialogs follow their page when the sidebar or window moves, and no longer pull Ardia in front of the app you're using. The eyedropper on design sites works.
- **Clearing a site clears all of it.** "Clear cookies & site data" now also removes the cookies a site sets for its whole domain, which usually hold your login, and the ones it left inside other sites. Downloads carry the page they came from again, and revealing a saved password or card always asks for Touch ID.

## New

- **Chrome's own Task Manager and Battery Saver.** Window › Task Manager opens Chrome's task manager, as Dia's does. Battery Saver now uses Chrome's tab freezing, which also eases the frame rate while you're on battery.
