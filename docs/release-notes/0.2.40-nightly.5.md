## New

- **A new engine under the same Chromium 155.** Ardia's engine now starts from Helium's privacy patches, by the Helium authors, instead of ungoogled-chromium's. Your profiles, tabs, passwords, cookies, bookmarks, history, extensions and uBlock Origin filters carry over as they are. A few defaults change with it:
  - Secure DNS switches on by itself when your DNS provider offers it.
  - A site that only works over plain HTTP asks first: a prompt says it doesn't support a secure connection and lets you continue.
  - Canvas, audio and CPU-core readouts get a little noise, so they're harder to use for fingerprinting.
- **Throw Picture in Picture to a corner.** A two-finger swipe or a quick fling sends the video window to the nearest corner of the screen, as in Dia.

## Fixed

- **英数 and かな switch the input source again.** In the New Tab bar and every other field of Ardia's own, the two Japanese keyboard keys were typed as keys instead of switching input, so a field that had once been Japanese stayed Japanese.
- **New tabs no longer flash white.** In dark mode a new tab showed a fully white page until the site painted. It now shows the tab's own background until then.
- **A rare crash after quick clicks is gone.** Two clicks handed over at once on the same sidebar row could end in an error that stopped Ardia's interface.
