## New

- **Profiles load when you open them.** At launch Ardia now starts only the profiles its windows show; the rest, and their extensions, start the first time you switch to them. With eight profiles, launch memory drops from about 1.5 GB to 390 MB. The first switch to a profile that hasn't started yet takes about two seconds; switches after that are as fast as before. Turn it off in Settings › Advanced › Performance › "Load profiles when opened". Thanks, [@Neved4](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/802).
- **WebKit pages block ads and trackers.** Pages opened in WebKit (streaming sites, and any site with a WebKit rule) now use uBlock Origin Lite's lists, and follow the same switches as the rest of Ardia: blocking on or off, the lists you picked, and the sites you trust. Site Controls shows how many requests it blocked.

## Fixed

- **A big bookmarks bar no longer freezes Ardia.** With a few thousand bookmarks and the bar set to Always, importing hung for minutes, every tab switch stalled, and the » menu could crash the app. Importing 2,500 bookmarks now takes half a second, and tab switches are instant.
- **Top Apps stay in their own profile.** In 0.2.44, profiles that share browsing data got every profile's Top Apps merged into each one, and edits in one showed up in the others. Sharing Top Apps is now off unless a profile turns it on in Settings › Profiles. Tiles that were merged stay where they are; delete the ones you don't want and they stay deleted.
- **A second Mac joining sync lists the first Mac's profiles.** If some of the first Mac's files were still downloading from iCloud when the second joined, the second never read them again and showed none of its profiles.
- **YNAB and other sites with fraud checks load again.** They showed "Something went wrong" on every load, because Ardia's screen-sharing code didn't look like Chrome's to their checks. No page can open the screen-sharing picker without a click either, which also stops the screen-recording permission prompts some pages set off in the background.
- **Picture in Picture works on YouTube TV.** Its player tells the browser a video can't go into Picture in Picture; Ardia's Picture in Picture button and auto Picture in Picture now open it anyway.
- **Tiling window managers leave Picture in Picture alone.** With AeroSpace, the Picture in Picture window got pulled back to Ardia's workspace on every switch and stuttered. It now floats over every workspace.
- **In full screen the window buttons hide until you need them.** They show with the menu bar when the pointer reaches the top of the screen, as in Arc and other Mac apps.
- **A window dragged to another display stays where you drop it, and snaps to its edges.** On macOS 27 it slid down or shrank after the drop, and dragging it to a screen edge didn't tile it.
- **Signing in from an extension opens its own window.** Stylus's Google Drive, Dropbox and OneDrive sync, and other extensions that sign in this way, put the sign-in page among your tabs and closed your window when it finished.
- **Top Apps tiles look as they did in 0.2.43.** In 0.2.44 they drew noticeably whiter, with a brighter edge.
- **Ardia opens again on early builds of macOS 26.** It crashed at launch while setting up the glass behind the sidebar.
- **The back and forward history menu opens beside a peeking sidebar.** With the sidebar hidden and the address bar in it, the menu opened underneath the sidebar, mostly cut off, and the sidebar slid away as you moved to it.
- **A tab's preview card goes away when you move onto a group's header.** It stayed on screen until you clicked.

## Smaller

- Links that open a new tab now open inside Little Ardia by default; "Open new-tab links inside Little Ardia" in Settings › General turns it off.
- Help › Copy Diagnostics now says which window has keyboard focus, to track down hover and typing that stop working after a while.
