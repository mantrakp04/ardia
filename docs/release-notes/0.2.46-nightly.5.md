## New

- **Auto Picture in Picture when you switch apps.** With Auto Picture in Picture on, going to another app while a video plays pops it out, and coming back to Ardia puts it back in the page. It works in WebKit tabs too, and pops out faster than before.
- **A "New tab opened" pill.** When a link opens in a background tab, a pill in the page's top right takes you to it. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/1203).
- **Peek is on by default.** Links to other sites and links that open a new tab now open in a Peek, as in Arc. Existing settings stay as they are.
- **⌥⌘← and ⌥⌘→ switch profiles**, as in Dia and Arc. Thanks, [@narasaka](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/1150).

## Fixed

- **New Tab stays at the bottom of a long tab list.** With more tabs than the window could show, the row under the list shrank away to just its arrow, and scrolling never brought it back.
- **The Dock icon brings your window back while a video is in Picture in Picture.** With the browser window minimized, a click on the Dock did nothing; now it restores the window.
- **⌘T after closing the last tab keeps the command bar up.** It used to close the bar that had just appeared and leave an empty page.
- **Collapsed groups keep the tab you're on in view** after you switch away. Thanks, [@FedyaLight](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/1216).
- **A WebKit page no longer covers the tab you switched to.** Thanks, [@narasaka](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/1219).
- **Sidebar polish.** A tab's unread count sits at the end of its row, a tab in Picture in Picture shows a PiP icon instead of a badge, profile initials and emoji sit centered, and a closed tab's title no longer lingers over another row. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/1203).
- **The link preview moves out of the way.** Near the bottom of a page, the link address shows at the top instead of covering footer links. Thanks, [@luangjokaj](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/1203).
- **Hovering a Settings search result no longer drags the list around.**
