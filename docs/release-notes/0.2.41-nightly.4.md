## New

- **Ardia runs on Chromium 156.** The engine moves up from 155 to 156.0.8078.47, with Helium 0.20.1's privacy patches on top, and brings Chrome 156's security fixes. Your profiles, tabs, passwords, cookies, extensions and uBlock Origin filters carry over as they are.
- **Streaming sites open in WebKit from the first visit.** Netflix, Disney+, Max, Prime Video, Hulu, Apple TV+, Spotify and others go straight to WebKit, where their protected video plays; Settings › Engines › Open streaming sites in WebKit turns it off. When a page moves to WebKit for protected video, **Move Back** returns it, and the WebKit prompt has **Don't ask again for this site**.
- **The selected tab's glow can be Full, Soft or Off** (Settings › Appearance › Selected Tab).
- **Page scroll bars can be thin**, even with a mouse plugged in (Settings › Appearance › Scroll Bars).
- **Tab groups have a None colour** that draws no box, like Arc's folders. Thanks, [@mtbaqer](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/179).
- **Private windows say they're private**, with an Incognito heading, a private search field and a sidebar badge. Thanks, [@nanadotam](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/322).
- **Profile themes follow dark mode**, so spaces imported from Arc as Light no longer stay light at night. Thanks, [@nanadotam](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/187).
- **Tabs in pinned groups keep their pinned page**, with Back to Pinned URL, and it syncs across your Macs. The tab menu is shorter, with moves and pinned-page controls in submenus. Thanks, [@TechSupportz](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/296).
- **The New Tab button's place is its own setting again**, separate from where new tabs open.
- **Windows open and close with a short fade**, like Little Ardia. Thanks, [@TechSupportz](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/271).
- **Popup windows show the site's address** in a strip the page can't cover. Thanks, [@aryankeluskar](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/261).

## Fixed

- **Closing a tab lets the page save first**, so Discord and other sites no longer sign you out when you close their tab.
- **Shortcut hints show the key you set.** Menus, the command bar and tooltips follow your rebinding, and Settings no longer reports a conflict with a shortcut you took over.
- **A pinned tab dragged in the top bar's dock lands where you drop it.**
- **Spotify in WebKit works with the sidebar player**: play, pause, previous and next reach the page, and the player shows up after you switch tabs.
- **The "Play in WebKit?" prompt only appears when protected video actually can't play**, so douyin.com and similar sites stop asking.
- **Launch looks settled from the first frame**: pinned icons, the glass and the address bar's colour no longer flicker in.
- **Hiding horizontal tabs (⌘S) slides them away**, and the page keeps its margins.
- **Arc's layout has Arc's thicker frame**, so the window is easier to grab. Thanks, [@narasaka](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/348).
- **The window-sharing pill stays with the window** when the sidebar is hidden. Thanks, [@narasaka](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/346).
- **Translation covers long footnotes and side-by-side buttons** on pages like apple.com.
- **Arc imports get Arc's grain**, about five times subtler than before.
- **Switching tabs shows the new page at once** instead of fading between them.

## Smaller

- The selected first tab's glow isn't cut off at the top of the list. Thanks, [@midverm](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/314).
- Closing a tab in a group leaves the sidebar where it was.
- With horizontal tabs, a tab picked from the command bar opens where Add new tabs says.
- The pinned-tab highlight setting works with tabs on top.
- The command bar's typed text matches its suggestions' size.
- The page resizes under the address bar once it has slid in, so buttons pinned to the bottom of a page stay in place.
- Move Tab Up and Down step over whole groups and stay out of the pinned area.
- The top tab strip scrolls to the selected tab when it's out of view.
- A site without an icon no longer shows the previous site's icon.
