headline: Chromium 155 arrives, and the battery hardly notices.

## New

- **Ardia runs on Chromium 155, built the way Chrome ships it.** The engine moves up from 154 and is now an official build with link-time and profile-guided optimisation, like Chrome's own. The window you left in front appears the moment Ardia launches, before the rest of the interface loads: about 290 ms against Helium's 380. Switching tabs from the sidebar is quicker too.
- **Small videos look sharper.** A video shown larger than its source is upscaled on the GPU, with Apple's super resolution at 720p and below. It only runs in the tab you're watching. It's on by default; "Video upscaling" in Settings › Tabs turns it off.

## Fixed

- **Ardia uses much less battery while idle.** A few minutes after launch, uBlock Origin's first save made Ardia measure everything uBlock stores, one burst that cost most of an idle hour's energy; it doesn't anymore, and uBlock no longer opens a hidden page at launch. Background tabs past the five most recent now freeze, as in Chrome, and pages that play sound or follow the system theme wake Ardia less: with 20 tabs open it wakes about 15 times a second, against Helium's 37.
