## New

- **Closing a tab goes back to the tab you used before it**, as in Arc. It used to jump to a neighbour or to whatever the page had opened. A sleeping tab isn't woken while an awake one is there to go back to. Thanks, [@maciekzygmunt](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/416).
- **Back on a tab a link opened closes it** and returns you to the tab that opened it. Thanks, [@avoid-ashraful](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1401).
- **⌃Tab shows each tab as a picture of its page**, in a row across the middle of the window, with its icon and title. Sleeping tabs are left out, so letting go never wakes one. Thanks, [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1486).
- **⌘T then Return goes back to your most recent tab** when New tab opens the command bar, and ↓ / ↑ move through the tabs without filling the field with their address. Thanks, [@Bil0000](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1355).
- **Put links from other apps first**, in Settings › Tabs, puts links from Slack, Mail and the rest just below your pinned tabs. Thanks, [@maciekzygmunt](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/803).
- **Swipe back to close Little Ardia** when its page has nothing to go back to. Settings can turn its gestures off, for anyone whose pinch fights page zoom.
- **Tidy works on the tabs below it and leaves your pins alone**, as in Arc. Thanks, [@torztomasz](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/410).
- **Live folders: hide a pull request** from its row's hover button, and with Show Completed off, a merged pull request's tab leaves the folder too. Thanks, [@torztomasz](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/418).
- **The clean-up suggestion has Don't show again**, a shorter card, and Undo. It used to come back every launch. Thanks, [@torztomasz](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1336).

## Fixed

- **⌘T on an empty New Tab page opens another tab again.** It focused that page's field instead, so you could only make one new tab at a time. Settings › Tabs › Reuse an empty New Tab page brings the old way back.
- **⌘S hides the tabs in its own window only**, as in Dia. It hid them in every window. Thanks, [@maciekzygmunt](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1619).
- **Esc closes Little Ardia** when the page doesn't have the keyboard, and a blank one closes from its address bar.
- **⌘Y puts the cursor in History's search field**, and what you type right after it lands there. Thanks, [@Josephh310](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1510).
- **Undo after cleaning up more than 100 tabs brings every one back.** Thanks, [@torztomasz](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1529).
- **Delete on an inline completion keeps the caret after what you typed.** Thanks, [@narasaka](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1270).

## Smaller

- **Tabs opened without a click paint 200 ms sooner**, such as those from an extension or a script.
- **Renaming a tab in the sidebar lines its text up** with the other rows. Thanks, [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1456).
- **The back and forward buttons fade into each other** as history changes. Thanks, [@araeyn](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1482).
- **`ardia browse set viewport` works again**, and its off-screen tabs stay out of the windows restored after a quit. A crashed tab it uses recovers on the next reload.
