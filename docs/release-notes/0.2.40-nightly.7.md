## New

- **Peek at a link before you open it.** Shift-click a link (or Option-click, set in Settings › Tabs) and its page opens in a small Little Ardia window over the one you're on. Open it as a tab with ⌘O or a two-finger spread, or close it with Esc, a pinch or a click outside. A Peek isn't saved, synced or kept in Recently Closed. Thanks, [@extraes](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/12).
- **Pinned folders, in the sidebar and on top.** Hold a pinned tile over another to make a folder tile, or pick New Folder with Tab from its menu. Groups can hold groups now, Arc's folders import as them, and with tabs on top pinned groups and nested folders show in the strip. Thanks, [@YishenTu](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/39).
- **Drag pinned tabs, groups and live folders into any order**, and the order follows you to your other Macs. Thanks, [@YishenTu](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/40).

## Fixed

- **A zoomed window fills itself again.** After a double-click on the window's top edge or Window › Zoom, the sidebar and page could stay at their old size in the top-left corner, and the empty rest of the window wouldn't drag. A layout from before the zoom could land after the new one.
- **The Picture in Picture button works on silent videos and on pages with several videos again**, and only for your own click or key press.
- **The hidden sidebar stays out while the pointer is on the Dock**, as in Arc. Thanks, [@narasaka](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/19).
- **The "Set Ardia as default browser" checkbox in onboarding can be clicked again.** Thanks, [@torztomasz](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/43).
- **Pinned tiles keep their 6 pt gaps after a drag**, and the rows no longer jitter while you reorder. Thanks, [@midverm](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/81).
- **Importing from Arc again adds nothing twice.** Thanks, [@YishenTu](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/38).
