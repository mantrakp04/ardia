## Fixed

- **Windows and tabs come back as you left them.** Windows that were in full screen return in full screen, each in its own Space, after a relaunch or an update. Tabs keep their Back and Forward history across a relaunch, and Back turns off after Clear History.
- **Little Ardia shows extension popups**, and popup windows, such as a Messenger call, can ask for the camera and microphone.
- **Arc import keeps favorites that are also pinned**, and onboarding no longer pins a site twice. Thanks, [@nanadotam](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/180).

## New

- **Ardia sends one anonymous "opened today" count a day, even with sharing off.** It holds the app version, update channel, macOS version, chip, and whether it's this copy's first day, week or month. There's no persistent ID; each day's count gets a new random one. Settings › Privacy & Security › What's Sent lists it.

## Smaller

- In full screen, the traffic lights move below the menu bar and stay clickable.
- Imported tab icons no longer turn into globes after 30 days.
- ⌘S in a page's text field goes to the page, and ⌘S twice quickly toggles the sidebar.
- A sidebar group shows its box only while the pointer is over it.
- A nested group sits a row gap below its parent's header. Thanks, [@YishenTu](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/137).
- The address bar pastes plain text, so a copied link pastes as its URL, with line breaks folded as in Chrome.
