## New

- **The full uBlock Origin is built in.** Ardia now ships uBlock Origin itself in place of uBlock Origin Lite, the way Helium does. Your allowed sites, your list choices and blocking-off carry over the first time it starts. Its filter lists keep themselves up to date, and the extension is half the size it was. At launch the first pages load while it starts, as in Helium; everything after that is blocked.
- **A second profile no longer runs down the battery.** The page left open in another profile now rests like a background tab until you swipe or switch to it, and wakes before it shows. A muted video playing there used to cost 70–150 mW all day; now the whole browser draws about 24.

## Fixed

- **Switching tabs is quicker.** A click on a tab in the sidebar shows it in about 5 ms instead of 13, and with 20 tabs open Ardia uses about half the idle CPU it did.
- **New windows open as Ardia windows, and a link from another app loads once.** A window a page or an extension opens is now a real Ardia window, and a link opened from another app while Ardia starts is fetched once instead of twice.
