## New

- **Web apps show their unread count on their tab.** Pinned tabs, Top Apps and ordinary tabs pick it up from the page title, the Badging API or a dot on the favicon.
- **Agents read and loop through pages in one step.** `ardia browse read` reads every matching link on a page through your logins, and `ardia browse script` runs a short loop across pages, so agents take fewer turns and far fewer tokens. A typeahead or guest field takes its values in one `select`, and an agent can tell us what got in its way with `ardia feedback`.
- **Agents can use your Mac's apps too.** `ardia computer` sets up Cua Driver, which works in background windows with a cursor of its own; macOS asks you for its permissions once.
- **Links from pinned tabs and Top Apps open in a Peek.** A link you click to another site opens over the app instead of in a new tab, with Gmail, Slack and Outlook redirect wrappers unwrapped. "Open new-tab links in" picks New tab, Peek or Little Ardia.
- **The page frame goes down to edge to edge.** A slider in Settings › Appearance and in onboarding.
- **Drop files, links or text on the New Tab page** to open them in that tab, from the Downloads popover too.
- **Bookmarks can hide, and Copy URL takes the star's place.** The command bar also pulses in the site's colour when you Tab into a site search.
- **Smaller touches.** A tab with a Peek open shows the Peek's icon, a pinned tab away from its address shows "/ title", shared Top Apps stay put when you switch spaces, the audio indicator holds for 2 seconds, and a same-site page swap keeps the old frame instead of flashing white.

## Fixed

- **Apple Store guest checkout works.** Apple's and the Education Store's checkout no longer end on "Page Not Found".
- **Crashes.** Ardia no longer crashes at launch with thousands of tabs, while loading certain images, when closing a window or moving a tab into or out of one, on an extension's popup with no tab, or on Cast remoting.
- **Video upscaling no longer stripes AV1 video** on M1 and M2 Macs.
- **A click that drifts a few points still counts,** on a tab and on a split pane.
- **The hover card in the top strip sits under it** and never blocks the other tabs.
- **Holding a button near the window's edge no longer opens the sidebar.**
- **Closing two windows that share tabs closes both,** and the close warning counts only the tabs that will close.
- **An agent never acts on your tab without being handed it.** Its own reads and inspections open tabs of its own, and `close` leaves your tabs alone.
- **Also fixed:**
  - the sync recovery kit error;
  - re-saving a password;
  - focus in an extension's side panel;
  - a flash on shared Top Apps;
  - the Dock icon under Tinted and Clear;
  - Picture in Picture on a second display.

## Crash reports

All of this is sent only when Settings › Privacy & Security › "Share anonymous crash reports and usage stats" is on.

- **A page, GPU or helper crash is now reported like an app crash:** the process type, how it ended and the stack, never the error message.
- **A crash report includes a little context from just before it:** how many tabs, windows and profiles were open, memory use, free disk (rounded) and how long the session ran.
- **Crashes noticed only after an update now arrive,** credited to the version that crashed, and a crash right after launch is sent within two seconds.
- **Each launch reports your Mac's chip, GPU, memory, displays and which video formats it decodes in hardware,** so playback bugs can be traced to hardware.
- **Hourly counts of tab clicks that turned into drags or got no response.** Counts only: no positions, no pages.
- **Turning sharing off now also erases anything collected but not yet sent.**

## Smaller

- Gemini in Chrome (glic) is off entirely.
