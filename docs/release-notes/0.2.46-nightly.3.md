## Fixed

- **A selected tab near the bottom of a long list keeps its whole glow.** Switching to a tab at the end of the sidebar left its glow cut flat at the list's bottom edge, most visibly on the last tab of a group. The sidebar now scrolls the tab far enough up for its glow to show, as it already did at the top.
- **The Downloads button sits evenly in the sidebar's corner.** With the address bar in the sidebar, the button in the footer sat 4 pt further from the left edge than from the bottom. It's now the same distance from both.

## New

- **A collapsed group keeps the tabs you opened from it.** Tabs opened while a group is collapsed stay under its header until you close them, as in Arc; clicking the header hides them, and the next click expands the group.
