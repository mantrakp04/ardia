## New

- **The glass is clear again, and quieter**: pinned tiles, fields and buttons go back to the clear glass from 0.2.43-nightly.7 at half its strength, so the edge and shine are there without the glow. Downloads and the site controls panel are half as see-through.
- **Glass means glass everywhere**: choosing Glass in Settings › Appearance now covers Downloads and the site controls panel too, and Classic keeps everything flat. The separate "Use glass for panels" switch is gone.
