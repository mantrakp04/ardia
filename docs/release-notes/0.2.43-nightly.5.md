## New

- **One clear glass across the app on macOS 26**: the address field, buttons, pinned tiles, hover cards, panels, Downloads and the New Tab cards share the same liquid glass, with no outlines.

## Fixed

- **Closing your last tab keeps the window** while it still has pinned tabs or a pinned group, as in Arc. A window with nothing pinned still closes and asks first.
- **Switching between Stable and Nightly keeps your downloaded update.** It's no longer hidden or deleted along the way, and quitting waits for it 8 seconds at most.
