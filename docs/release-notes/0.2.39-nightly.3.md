date: 2026-10-20
headline: Scrolling keeps up again, on battery too.
---

## Fixed

- **Scrolling runs at full speed on battery.** With Battery Saver on, Ardia capped every page at 30 frames a second whenever the Mac ran on battery, scrolling and typing included. Scrolling, typing and clicking now lift that cap until a second after you stop; Battery Saver's other savings stay.
- **Things that follow the scroll no longer shake.** Since 0.2.38, sticky headers, parallax and other content a site moves as you scroll updated at half the rate of the scroll itself. They keep up now, while idle pages and video still save power at 60 frames a second.

## Smaller

- Matching the sidebar's colour to the page no longer costs work on every scrolled frame.
