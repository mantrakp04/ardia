## New

- **Set Ardia up from files**: settings, keybindings and themes can live in `~/.config/ardia/config.json` and `themes/<name>.json`, dotfiles style. Ardia applies them at launch and on every save, and never writes them back. With "Let the ardia command control Ardia" on, the `ardia` command can also run commands, list windows and run your own programs when tabs and windows change.
- **Custom themes from the New Tab page**: "Make it yours" now has the Custom swatch and the gradient editor from Settings, and the onboarding window shows a custom gradient instead of one flat tint.
- **The sidebar player shows the time left** where its pause button was, ticking while it plays. Pause is one hover away; a paused player keeps its play button.

## Fixed

- **A muted tab's notifications are silent**: muting a pinned mail or chat tab, or its site, now also mutes the sound of its notifications.
- **The collapsed sidebar stays put while another app is in front**: a hover over Ardia's edge from the background no longer slides it out later, and the floating sidebar now lines up with the page at the top and bottom.

## Smaller

- A tab group's box is as tight below its last tab as above its header.
- In the Glass style, the omnibox's Go and Dictate buttons are liquid glass.
