## Fixed

- **Dragging a tab in the auto-hidden sidebar reorders it.** With tabs on auto-hide, dragging a tab inside the floating sidebar always made a split view, and the sidebar could slide away mid-drag. A drop on the list now moves the tab, as in the docked sidebar; drag it out over the page to split. Esc cancels a tab drag.
