---
date: 2026-10-24
headline: Twenty-odd small things now do what they said.
---

## New

- **Group pinned tabs.** Pinned rows now have New Group with Tab and Move to Group, ⌃⌘N groups them, and dropping a tab on the middle of a pinned row makes a pinned group in its place.
- **Group tabs by dragging with tabs on top.** Drop a tab on the middle of another to make a group, or on a group to add it. A drop near the edge still reorders.

## Fixed

- **Typing an accent no longer crashes Ardia.** On keyboards with dead keys, like Portuguese, typing ´ or ` in a tab name, the command bar or the find bar closed the app.
- **The speaker shows on tabs playing sound again**, including YouTube videos that start slowly or in a background tab.
- **Page translation is faster**, and translating a page again, or a second tab, is nearly instant.
- **Install and Relaunch works** when a window is full screen, even on another Space. **Switching to Stable drops a nightly** that was already downloaded, so it doesn't install on quit.
- **Window buttons stay in the sidebar in full screen**, as in Dia.
- **Windows keep their size** after Zoom or tiling, and a new window opens at the last one's size.
- **Little Ardia opens on the display you clicked the link on.** Thanks, [@0xJeu](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/issues/75).
- **A Top Apps folder stays lit while you hold a tile over it,** so the tile joins it instead of the folder sliding away.
- **The floating sidebar stays out** while you use its menus, a tab's hover card, a rename or the icon picker. **Site Controls opens beside it** instead of behind it.
- **The ⌘T bar opens at its full width**, without growing in.
- **The theme editor shows the theme you picked**, in the window behind it and in its preview. Thanks, [@hernan0078](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/59).
- **Cleaned tabs leave Recently Cleaned after a week**, and the count beside New Tab only shows where it fits.
- **A pull request's hover card no longer overflows.** Thanks, [@0xJeu](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/88).
- **Chrome's "Close window with last tab: Never" flag works.**
- **No more empty extra profiles** after accepting an extension that another app installed, and that extension no longer pops up a prompt.
- **Arc import brings your sidebar even with a Little Arc open**, and GitHub and Bitbucket live folders come over as live folders. Profiles made from one Arc profile's spaces share its data.
- **No more crash reports from Apple's Passwords helper.** Ardia no longer starts it, since it only runs in browsers Apple recognises.
