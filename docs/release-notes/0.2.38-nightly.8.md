## Faster

- **Your tabs come back sooner.** After a restart, the page you left open shows about 40 ms earlier, and new tabs paint about 10 ms sooner. The window itself is on screen about 25 ms earlier too.
- **Video and background tabs use less power.** Pages draw their JavaScript animation at 60 frames a second on 120 Hz screens, while scrolling, typing, video and CSS animation stay at full speed. Quiet tabs in the background wake less often; tabs with a live connection, playing audio or allowed to notify you never freeze.

## Smaller

- **The download is 23% smaller and the app 20% smaller.** Ardia no longer ships the parts of Chrome it never loads: its other languages, its software graphics fallback and its first-run sounds.
