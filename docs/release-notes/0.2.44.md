## New

- **Sidebar on the right**: pick Right under the layout cards in Make it yours, in Settings › Tabs › Sidebar position, or with View › Sidebar on Right. The window buttons move with it, and the hidden sidebar peeks in from the right and stays open while you look at it.
- **Share a pinned folder through iCloud Drive**: right-click it and choose Share Folder… to keep it in `iCloud Drive › Ardia Shared` and share it with macOS's own share sheet. No account needed. Links sync both ways, per profile, and open from Finder too.
- **Profiles that share browsing data can share their Top Apps**, as Arc's spaces share Favorites. Pin, rename, reorder or folder a tile in one and it changes in all, and each tile stays one live page across profiles. Each profile has its own switch in Settings › Profiles › Shared data, in its details and in its right-click menu. Thanks, [@ymehili](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/136).
- **Set Ardia up from files**, dotfiles style: settings, keybindings and themes can live in `~/.config/ardia/config.json` and `themes/<name>.json`. Ardia applies them at launch and on every save, and never writes them back. The same file can add your own address bar rows, from a list, a URL template or a suggest endpoint.
- **Hooks**: rules in `~/.config/ardia/hooks.json` can rewrite or block links before they open, send links from a given app to a profile, file finished downloads into a folder, deny camera or microphone requests, or run a command when something happens. Anything that reaches outside the page you're on waits until you approve it in Settings › Advanced › Automation.
- **The `ardia` command can drive Ardia**: with "Let the ardia command control Ardia" on, it runs commands, lists windows, and `ardia browse` takes agent-browser's commands and runs them on the Ardia you have open.
- **A real emoji picker** for profile and sidebar icons: search, every emoji by category, and SF Symbols, with the current icon highlighted.
- **Share from the page menu**: right-click to share selected text, an image, a link (trackers removed) or the page. File › Share › Share… is now ⌥⌘S.
- **Custom themes from the New Tab page**: "Make it yours" now has the Custom swatch and the gradient editor from Settings.
- **The sidebar player shows the time left** where its pause button was, ticking while it plays. Pause is one hover away.
- **New-tab links can stay in Little Ardia**: turn on Settings › General › "Open new-tab links inside Little Ardia" and a link that would open a new tab loads in Little Ardia itself, so Back takes you back. Thanks, [@narasaka](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/344).
- **A pull request folder can hide repos.** Its menu has a Repositories submenu, as Arc's does. Thanks, [@maciekzygmunt](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/309).
- **Picture in Picture's corners line up with the window's**: thrown into a corner, its curve nests inside the browser window's rounded corner.

## Fixed

- **Pages scroll smoothly with the sidebar open in the Glass style.** Every Top Apps tile cost its own pass each frame, and scrolling stuttered. The tiles now render in one.
- **Typing some words with dashes and dots no longer freezes the address bar.** Text like `my-post.another-post.x:` stalled each keystroke for seconds and could crash Ardia.
- **Three more crashes are gone**: the green button's Move & Resize and Fill & Arrange menus on macOS 27, any page that started a service worker while an `ardia` session was connected, and running low on disk space.
- **Picture in Picture works in WebKit tabs**, including for a video embedded in another site's player, and its Back to Tab takes you to the video when you were in a split view.
- **The top tab strip scrolls without sliding under the window buttons**, closing a tab keeps the next tab's × under the pointer, and a split tab opens wherever you click it.
- **Holding a swipe back opens the history list upward**, as Dia's does, with the nearest page at the bottom under your fingers.
- **The collapsed sidebar stays put while another app is in front**: a hover over Ardia's edge from the background no longer slides it out later.
- **A muted tab's notifications are silent**: muting a pinned mail or chat tab, or its site, also mutes the sound of its notifications.
- **Names save when you click away**, so a profile name no longer needs Return.
- **The sidebar's address field on a New Tab page** goes straight to the page's own search box when ⌘L opens in the centre, instead of stacking a second box on top.
- **Expanding Little Ardia while a link is still loading** no longer leaves the tab on a blank page. Thanks, [@narasaka](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/551).
- **The peeked sidebar's traffic lights respond again** when the address bar is in the toolbar. Thanks, [@narasaka](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/771).
- **Tabs moved to another window** land where "New tabs open at" puts a new tab, and never inside a group. Thanks, [@daberni](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/730).

## Smaller

- Text sizes, corners, spacing and animation timing across the app now come from one small set of values, and look-alike controls are the same piece everywhere.
- A tab group's box is as tight below its last tab as above its header, and its last row rounds to match.
- In the Glass style, the omnibox's Go and Dictate buttons are liquid glass.
- The pinned group's emoji and a folder's icons in the top strip aren't cut off anymore.
- Extension buttons stay quiet when the extension is already gone.
- The bundled uBlock Origin is now 1.75.4.
