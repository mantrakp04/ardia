## New

- **Air Traffic Control.** Settings › Air Traffic Control sends links from other apps to the profile you pick, as a
  new tab, a new window or Little Ardia. A rule matches by app and by site or pattern, the first one that fits
  decides, and Everything Else catches the rest. Try a Link shows where a link would go before one arrives.
- **Split View marks the pane you're in.** A ring in the profile's colour outlines the focused side, and follows the
  window's rounded corners when there's no page frame.
- **Buttons answer a press.** Toolbar, sidebar, media and download controls give a small press motion, and the reload
  and stop glyphs sit centred.
