## New

- **Glass or Classic** in Settings › Appearance and in setup: Classic brings back Dia's flat look everywhere; Glass can take on your profile's colour, with a Tint amount slider, and the site controls panel and Downloads can be glass too. The glass itself is quieter, with far less glow on the pinned tiles and fields, and the top tab strip's pinned tabs are glass as well.
- **Setup's custom colour has the full theme options**: pass-through blur and noise, alongside intensity and direction.

## Fixed

- **⌘N opens the new window on the profile you're in**, instead of always the first one. The sidebar player grows smoothly on hover instead of jumping half open, the sidebar address field has the same glass edge as the tiles, and download rows reach the panel's right edge.

## Smaller

- **If you share anonymous usage stats**, Ardia now also sends your on/off, pick-one and number settings once a day, each with its default, so we can tell which defaults people change. Never addresses, names, search engines or anything you type; What's Sent lists it.
