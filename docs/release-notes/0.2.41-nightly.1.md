## New

- **New tabs can open below the current one.** Settings › Tabs › "Add new tabs" has a third choice, "Below the current tab": the new tab joins that tab's group, and closing it takes you back.
- **Screen sharing can include audio.** For a screen or a window, tick "Also share system audio"; for a window, the app's own audio when the site asks for it. Sharing a tab carries its sound again.
- **Remove a closed pin with a second middle-click or its ✕**, as Arc's "Archive this tab" does, with Undo. This also lets you close tabs in pinned groups, which before stayed put.
- **⌥-click can open a link in split view**, a new choice in Settings › Tabs › Links. ⌥⇧-click and ⌥⌘-click open the split again, as in Dia, instead of a new window or a plain tab.
- **Hold ⌘ in the profile menu to open a profile in a new window.** Thanks, [@felipeorlando](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/115).
- **New installs get a new onboarding**: Arc or Dia first, then one choice per page, previewed as you pick.

## Fixed

- **Focusing the address bar with Zhuyin or Kotoeri no longer crashes Ardia.**
- **Clear keeps the selected tab and tabs playing sound**, as in Arc, instead of closing everything and opening a New Tab page. Undo works until you change the window.
- **Ardia is sharp on 1x external displays.** Pages and the sidebar looked soft when the Mac's own screen was the main one.
- **Closing a pane of a pinned split keeps it one pinned split**, instead of splitting it into separate pins.
- **Settings › Extensions opens on the window's profile**, so a pin you turn on shows in that window, and Back returns to the Extensions list.
- **Import finds Brave Beta and Nightly**, Edge's and Vivaldi's other channels too, and brings tabs over from browsers on Chromium 156.

## Smaller

- Tab menus say where a tab goes, as Dia does: "Move to Top Apps" and "Move to Pinned in", then the profile's name.
- Tab groups can have no icon.
- The profile dots show Dia's tooltip.
