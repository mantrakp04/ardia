## New

- **A compact ⌘T, as a plugin.** Before you type, the command bar can skip your pinned tabs and Top Apps and list fewer open tabs. Install it with one line from [ardia-plugins](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia-plugins), or set `commandBarStart` in config.json yourself. Typing still finds every tab.

## Fixed

- **Content blocker toggles stay where you put them.** After switching panes or profiles, the switches could slide back to off while uBlock Origin reloaded its lists. Settings now saves them right away, and says so when the blocker isn't answering.
