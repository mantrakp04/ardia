## New

- **Organize tabs and groups from the command line.** `ardia tabs` and `ardia groups` list every window, space, group and tab as JSON, open pages into a group at a given spot, move tabs in and out of groups, create, nest, rename, recolour and give groups an icon, and pin, mute, sleep or close tabs, all without changing the tab you're on. `ardia apply` runs a batch in one go, so you or an agent can rebuild a whole workspace.
- **Report a Problem, one sheet for everything.** Help › Report a Problem… (⌥⇧⌘I) takes bugs, slowness and ideas, with an optional recording of Ardia's window that never shows page contents, a short engine trace for slowness, and a preview of exactly what is sent.

## Fixed

- **Closing your last window keeps it, as in Arc.** Closing the only window with the red button or ⇧⌘W no longer asks "Close N tabs?" and throws your unpinned tabs away: click Ardia in the Dock and the same window comes back, with its tabs in order, its groups, its pinned tabs and the tab you were on. Quitting with no window open and opening Ardia again does the same. ⌘N still opens a new window.
- **Picture in Picture stays up over full-screen apps.** Switching to an app in full screen left the video behind on the desktop; now it floats over the full-screen app, as Safari's and Arc's do, whether the app went full screen before or after the video popped out.
- **The profile switcher's icons have room.** With "Show profile icons instead of dots" on, the icons sat so close that a click often switched to the wrong profile; they now have the same breathing room as the dots and a wider target to click.
- **Auto-hide tabs remembers your choice.** Hiding your tabs used to last only for that window, so a new window, or a window opened fresh after an update, brought them back. Now it's a setting: Settings › Tabs › Auto-hide tabs, in either tab layout, and ⌘S changes it from any window. If every window had its tabs hidden before this update, the setting starts on.
- **Tabs across the top get a hide button.** With the top-tabs layout, the toolbar's first button now hides and shows the tab strip, and its tooltip says what it does, Hide Tabs or Show Tabs, in both layouts.
- **Full screen works like Arc's.** No more flickering traffic lights or empty gap in the sidebar: point at the top and macOS's own title bar slides down with them.
- **The hidden sidebar stops flickering at the window's corner,** and its Peek stays attached to the window's edge as it springs open.
- **Site panels close when you click elsewhere or press Esc,** and the ⌃Tab switcher lists only open tabs.
- **Also fixed:**
  - hover cards touching the page or covering other Top Apps tiles;
  - "Back to Pinned URL" sticking after you leave the pinned page again;
  - a selected Top Apps tile with a black icon showing no ring;
  - the camera dot staying on a slept or unloaded tab.
