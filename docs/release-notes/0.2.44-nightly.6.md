## New

- **Profiles that share browsing data share their Top Apps**, as Arc's spaces share Favorites. Pin, rename, reorder or folder a tile in one and it changes in all, and each tile stays one live page: open it in another profile and the same tab comes along, nothing reloads. Turn it off in Settings › Profiles › Shared data › Share Top Apps. Thanks, [@ymehili](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/136).
- **A pull request folder can hide repos.** Its menu has a Repositories submenu, as Arc's does, so a work profile's folder can leave out your personal repos. Thanks, [@maciekzygmunt](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/309).

## Fixed

- **Pages scroll smoothly with the sidebar open.** With Glass on, every Top Apps tile cost its own pass each frame, and scrolling stuttered. The tiles now render in one, a touch darker than before.
- **Holding a swipe back opens the history list upward**, as Dia's does, with the nearest page at the bottom under your fingers, and each step slides the list instead of a highlight.
- **Ardia no longer crashes while an `ardia` session is connected.** With "Let the ardia command control Ardia" on, any page that started a service worker took the whole browser down, often within a minute or two.
- **A sidebar on the right stays open when it peeks.** With the sidebar hidden, it used to slide in at the right edge and close again as soon as the pointer moved onto it, so you couldn't look at your tabs.
- **Picture in Picture's Back to Tab takes you to the video** when you were in a split view. It used to bring Ardia forward but leave the split on screen and pop the video back out a moment later.
