## Fixed

- **Reopening a window you just closed keeps Back.** When a page had something to do as it closed, as most web apps do, closing its window took four seconds behind the scenes, and a window reopened inside those seconds came back with its tabs but none of their history. A window now closes at once, so ⇧⌘T right after closing brings back each tab with Back and Forward. A private window's session also ends as its window closes, not seconds later.
- **Live folders, splits and Peek hold their place.** A live folder you made, renamed or marked read in the last second before ⌘Q is saved at quit. Adding a pane beside two stacked tabs no longer drops one of them from the split, and opening a Peek as a tab no longer shuffles pinned tabs.

## New

- **`ardia browse` gets an agent's job done in fewer steps.** An action now answers with the text of whatever it opened, `read --scroll` reads a whole long list in one go, `fill` edits spreadsheet cells and picks from menus, and a drag lands where you aimed it and says where that was. A button the page redrew keeps working under its old ref when there's no doubt which one it is.
