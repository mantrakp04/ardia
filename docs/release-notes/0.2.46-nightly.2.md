## Fixed

- **Reopening a window you just closed keeps Back.** Closing a window used to take four seconds behind the scenes, and a window reopened inside those seconds came back with its tabs but none of their history. A window now closes at once, so ⇧⌘T right after closing brings back each tab with Back and Forward. A private window's session also ends as its window closes, not seconds later.
- **Live folders remember what you did just before quitting.** A folder you made, renamed or marked read in the last second before ⌘Q could come back as it was. It's saved at quit now.
- **Splits, Peek and the extension side panel hold their place.** Adding a pane beside two stacked tabs no longer drops one of them from the split, opening a Peek as a tab no longer shuffles pinned tabs, and grabbing the side panel's edge while it settles no longer snaps it to the wrong width.
