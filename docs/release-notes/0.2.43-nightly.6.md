## New

- **Dia's flat look everywhere on macOS 26**: pinned tiles, buttons, the address field, the command bar, hover cards, popovers, Downloads, Peek, the New Tab cards and onboarding drop the liquid glass for Dia's faint fills and one-pixel outlines, measured against Dia itself.
- **Picture in Picture has its plain controls back**, without the glass plates.
- **"Highlight pinned tabs in their icon's colors" is gone from Settings**: the selected Top App always gets the ring in its icon's colors, as in Dia.
