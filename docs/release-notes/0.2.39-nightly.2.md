headline: Pinned tabs can be rows, not just tiles.
---

## New

- **Pin tabs as rows above your tabs.** As in Dia's Pinned section, a pinned tab can sit as a regular row between the tiles and your tabs, instead of a square. Right-click a tab and choose Move to Pinned Tabs, or drag it there; Move to Top Apps makes it a tile again. Double-clicking a pinned tile now takes it back to its pinned page, as in Dia; Rename is in its menu.
- **The tab hover card animates like Dia's.** It fades and springs in from its corner, and shrinks away when you move off.

## Fixed

- **Renaming a tab group works again.** The name field closed the moment it opened, so the old name stayed. The same went for renaming a single tab.
- **The sidebar's address field works on a New Tab page.** Clicking it did nothing; it now opens the address dropdown, as on any page.

## Smaller

- Opening a new tab no longer flashes its centred box over the page you were on.
- Pinned tiles keep their spacing after switching profiles from the menu.
- Each colour in a tab group's colour menu shows its swatch again.
- Search Tabs (⇧⌘A) opens every time, also with the command bar open.
