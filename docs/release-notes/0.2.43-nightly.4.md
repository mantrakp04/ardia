## New

- **Memory Saver replaces our tab sleeping**, as in Helium: Settings › Advanced › Performance turns it off, picks Moderate, Balanced or Maximum, and keeps chosen sites always active. It's on by default, and Chrome never puts a pinned tab or the tab you're on to sleep.
- **Zooming shows the zoom level.** ⌘+, ⌘- and ⌘0 open a small "Zoom: 110%" panel that closes on its own a moment later, so you can see when you're back at 100%.
- **Collapse a profile's pinned tabs**: click "Pinned" on the line above your tabs, or use the sidebar menu. The tab you're on stays in view, and each profile remembers it.
- **Links to other sites can open in Peek** from Top Apps and pinned tabs, as in Arc. Turn it on in Settings › Tabs › Links.
- **The bookmarks bar takes drag and drop**: reorder it, drop into and out of folders, or drop a tab on it. Folder menus show each site's icon, and Settings › Appearance has a Large size.
- **Mute a single tab.** Muting a tab no longer mutes every tab on that site; Mute Site is its own choice, behind ⌥ in the tab menu and in Site Controls.

## Fixed

- **Sync shows your other Mac's profiles.** A Mac that joined before the other Mac had sent its profiles could never get them; it now waits for them and lists each one with Add to This Mac, or Add All.
- **Extensions speak your Mac's language.** Chrome's pages and extensions followed English whatever your Mac was set to, and uBlock Origin's own text was blank.
- **Obsidian Web Clipper saves to your vault.** Clicking Open on its prompt did nothing.
- **Picture in Picture opens where you left it**, on the same monitor, instead of jumping back to the main screen.
- **Old copies of Ardia no longer show up** in the default browser and Open With lists.
- **Dragging the Settings window doesn't flicker.**

## Smaller

- The profile icons at the bottom of the sidebar are a little smaller.
- A website gets every key first, except the shortcuts Helium keeps for the browser, such as New Tab, Close Tab, Copy URL and Developer Tools.
