## New

- **Open a page in WebKit, so DRM video like Netflix plays.** Right-click a tab, or choose View › Open Page in WebKit. Site Controls › "Opens in" remembers the choice for a site, and Settings › Engines lists those rules. When a page's video needs DRM that Chromium can't play, Ardia offers to move it. WebKit keeps its own sign-ins, and extensions and the ad blocker stay in Chromium for now.
- **Import from Zen**, with each space as a profile, its pinned tabs, folders, Essentials and tab icons, plus bookmarks, history, cookies and passwords. Thanks, [@JustMarkDev](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/123).
- **Hovering a collapsed group shows every tab in it**, most recently used first, with a search field and arrow keys, as in Arc. Thanks, [@YishenTu](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/160).
- **Extensions show up in the command bar.** Type an extension's name to open its popup or its options, as in Arc. Thanks, [@thomasrene](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/issues/182), for asking.
- **Custom themes can be black, and their gradient can turn.** The colour wheel has a brightness bar, and a dial sets the gradient's direction. Thanks, [@maciekzygmunt](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/103).
- **Pinned tabs show − and unload**, as in Arc, and a pinned group's − unloads all its tabs. Closing an unpinned group asks first. Thanks, [@Blackajiro](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/64).
- **Live folders can hide a pull request until you refresh**, and each folder can turn off "Group Stacked Pull Requests" and "Show Completed". Thanks, [@torztomasz](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/72).
- **The Raycast extension can switch profiles and open new tabs and windows**, incognito ones included. Thanks, [@ridemountainpig](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/125).
- **Ardia installs with Homebrew**: `brew install mantrakp04/tap/ardia`, or `ardia@nightly` for nightlies.

- **Imported tabs show their icons before you open them**, read from the browser you import from. Thanks, [@0xJeu](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/71).
- **The page no longer jumps on every `ardia` command.** A pill floats over the page while the CLI is connected, instead of a bar that pushed it down. Thanks, [@om-ray](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/147).
- **Little Ardia fades in once its page is ready and fades out on close**, and you can drag it by its title. Thanks, [@TechSupportz](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/148).
- **The Downloads panel hangs from its button**, and the page shows blurred through it. Thanks, [@404khai](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/116).

- **Hovering a group below a collapsed live folder shows the group**, not one of the folder's pull requests. Thanks, [@YishenTu](https://github.com/mantrakp04/ardia/pull/137).
