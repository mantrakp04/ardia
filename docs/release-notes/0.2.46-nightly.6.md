## New

- **Onboarding is three steps.** Where you're coming from, how it looks, and a last few choices; everything else is set from the browser you picked, with Customize more… for the rest. It used to be eight steps and up to 24 screens. Helium is now found on your Mac and imports from there.
- **Redo Setup… in Settings › General** walks through onboarding again, starting from your current settings. Cancel at any step keeps things as they were.
- **A tab switches when the mouse button goes down**, as in Chrome, Safari and Arc. That takes about a tenth of a second off every switch. Dragging, ⌘-click and ⇧-click work as before.
- **Clicking the address edits it in place.** The suggestions drop down from the field; ⌘T still opens the bar in the middle. "In the centre" in Settings now applies to ⌘L only.
- **Unread counts on tabs are off by default**, and readable when you turn them on in Settings › Tabs: a calm pill with larger digits, and the title drops its own "(3)" so the count shows once. A switch you turned on yourself stays on.
- **⌘W closes every selected tab**, and the menu says Close 3 Tabs. Thanks, [@bwfront](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1165).
- **⌥↩ in the command bar opens the address in Split View.** Thanks, [@bwfront](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1142).
- **A borderless page frame**, and no frame in full screen when tabs are hidden. Thanks, [@bwfront](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/1181).

## Fixed

- **Watching upscaled video for a long time no longer crashes it.** After about 36 minutes at 30 fps, or 18 at 60, the video process could die. macOS's upscaler leaked one small object per frame until a system-wide pool ran out; Ardia now calls it a way that doesn't.
- **A profile keeps its extensions when the keychain key changes.** Every extension in the default profile could vanish at the next launch, with no message.
- **Search suggestions come from the browser itself.** They were fetched outside it, with a different identity and cookies, which may be why Google showed some people its "unusual traffic" page. Private windows no longer ask for suggestions at all.
- **Settings search finds what you typed.** "cli" now leads to the ardia command, not to every setting with "click" in it; matches are highlighted and the pane follows your selection.
- **Switching the update channel checks it at once** and says what it found. It used to download quietly, or not check at all, and Check Now stayed dimmed.
- **One switch for links from other apps.** "Open links from other apps in Little Ardia" is now a single toggle in Settings › General, and search finds it. The toggle people were turning off only applied inside a Little Ardia window.
- **Report a Problem keeps your draft through a quit**, recording included, retries by itself when the server is busy, and says which part failed instead of "upstream".
- **Glass or classic? shows the difference.** Both choices drew the same preview in onboarding.
- **The New Tab row stops looking highlighted** after you open a tab from it.
- **Site Controls opens next to its button with the sidebar on the right**, as do the zoom, permission and history popovers. They opened on the far side of the window.
- **⌃Tab ignores tabs you haven't looked at.** A tab opened in the background joins the recent order once it's shown.
- **An auto-hidden sidebar reopens only from the window's edge**, and keeps the theme's gradient while it peeks.
- **Auto Picture in Picture pops out only when the window is out of sight.** Switching to an app beside Ardia leaves the video in the page.
- **Allowing a site to use your location asks macOS for it**, so the site gets an answer instead of waiting.
- **Tabs in a group keep when they were last used** after Start fresh or a reopen. Thanks, [@YishenTu](https://github.com/ardia-browser/ardia/pull/584).
