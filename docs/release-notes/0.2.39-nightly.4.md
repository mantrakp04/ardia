headline: The sliders button grew a shelf for your extensions.

## New

- **Site Controls looks like Arc's, and pins extensions.** The sliders button in the address row opens a panel with Share, Copy Clean Link and Find on Page on top, your extensions as tiles, and the site's settings below. Right-click an extension to pin it to the toolbar or unpin it; ⋯ has everything the old list had.
- **You can hide the sidebar button.** Settings › Tabs › Show sidebar button. ⌘S still shows and hides the sidebar.
- **The margins around the page move the window.** Grab the space above, beside or below the page to drag the window, and double-click it to zoom, as Dia and Arc allow.

## Fixed

- **Switches turn off with a click on their knob.** Once a switch was on, clicking its knob did nothing, so Battery Saver and the rest took several clicks to turn off.
- **Battery Saver waits for a low battery.** It used to turn on whenever the Mac was unplugged, which made scrolling stutter on older builds. It now turns on at 20% or less, or in Low Power Mode, and stays on until you plug in. If you had turned it off, it stays off.
- **⌘L works on a New Tab page.** With the address bar in the sidebar, it opens the sidebar's address field, as on any page.
- **Backspace after "x.com " gives back "x.com ".** Typing a space after a site switches to searching it; Backspace now restores the space instead of dropping it, and no longer closes the command bar.
- **The downloads popover closes when you clear it.** It also animates closed, has even padding, and matches the size and type of the other popovers.

## Smaller

- Pinned tiles no longer show a return arrow after you navigate away; Reset is still in the hover card.
- Hovering a tab group's header no longer draws a second edge inside the group.
- With a narrow sidebar, back and forward stay side by side and reload gives way first.
- Clicking the middle of an extension's toolbar icon opens it; only its edge used to.
- The "+ New Tab" row no longer gets stuck over the list after opening several tabs at once.
- Popovers open a little faster and animate closed.
