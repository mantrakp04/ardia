## Faster

- **Switching tabs and opening a new one no longer slow down as tabs pile up.** Every row in the sidebar used to recheck itself on any change anywhere, and the amount it checked had grown release by release. Now only the rows that changed do any work: with 200 tabs a switch does about a third of the bookkeeping it did.

## Fixed

- **Importing from Zen puts pinned tabs in the right profile.** Pinned tabs, folders and Essentials from every Zen profile, workspace and container could all land in your first profile. Each now goes to its own, and workspaces that share a container share their data. Zen from before 1.18 is read too.
- **A Report a Problem recording shows Picture in Picture.** The mini player was missing from every recording. It is drawn where it sits now, blacked out when pages are hidden. A recording only ever holds its own window, never your other windows, and recording a private window always hides pages.
- **Onboarding fits a small window.** At the smallest size, rows were cut off in all three steps. The last step is one column now, without the gap in the middle.
- **Help fix Ardia? stays out of the way.** The card sat over the top-left of every New Tab page. It moved to the bottom-left corner and hides when the command bar needs the room.
- **Settings search shows one result per place.** "cli" listed the ardia command twice.
- **Check Now says Checking… while it checks**, with a spinner, instead of going dim.
