## Fixed

- **Keyboard focus stays where you left it.** Esc closes Find on page again, and Enter keeps you in the field, stepping to the next match (Shift+Enter goes back). Switching tabs with Ctrl+Tab, switching profiles or returning to a split puts the caret back in the field you were typing in, docked DevTools included. Recent Tabs no longer picks whatever row your resting pointer happens to sit on, and in the command bar the pointer only highlights a row: the arrow keys choose, as in Dia.
- **The lamb game runs at a steady 120 fps.** It used to redraw the whole flock every frame just to animate the hint ring, which kept the GPU busy and dropped the found and time's-up screens to about 60 fps.
- **Fewer ways for a page to fool you.** A page from another site can't paint before the address bar shows it, a blank tab can't pose as another address, and the Cast picker drops the page out of full screen first. uBlock Origin runs from the signed app and stays off local files and the Web Store, extensions Google flags as malware get turned off again, and the `ardia` command starts switched off and can no longer read cookies, open chrome:// or extension pages, or touch the clipboard.
