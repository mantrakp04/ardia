## New

- **Each profile picks whether it shares Top Apps.** Profiles that share browsing data used to share their Top Apps all together or not at all. Now each one has its own switch in Settings › Profiles › Shared data, in its details and in its right-click menu. A profile that opts out keeps its own tiles and pages.
- **Picture in Picture's corners line up with the window's.** Thrown into a corner, the PiP window's curve now nests inside the browser window's rounded corner instead of sitting off it.

## Fixed

- **The top tab strip scrolls without sliding under the window buttons.** With many tabs in horizontal mode, scrolling pushed Top Apps and pinned groups under the traffic lights. They're cut off at the strip's edge now. Closing a tab keeps the next tab's × under the pointer until you move away.
- **A split tab in the top strip opens wherever you click it.** Only clicks near its centre used to open it. The pinned group's emoji and a folder's icons aren't cut off anymore either.
- **Typing some words with dashes and dots no longer freezes the address bar.** Text like `my-post.another-post.x:` stalled each keystroke for seconds and could crash Ardia.
- **The green button's Move & Resize and Fill & Arrange menus no longer crash Ardia on macOS 27.** Ardia also stays up when the disk runs low, and extension buttons stay quiet when the extension is already gone.
