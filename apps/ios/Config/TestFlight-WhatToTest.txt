Thanks for testing Ardia for iPhone. It needs iOS 27.

Things to try:
- Search from the start screen and open a few pages.
- Swipe the bottom bar to switch tabs. Open the tab deck and flick a card up to close it.
- The page menu (the chevron): Reader, Find on Page, Night Shade, Page Zoom.
- Settings > Color: pick a color and check that the whole app follows it, in light and dark.
- If you use Ardia on a Mac: on the Mac, Settings > Sync > Connect Another Device shows a code. Scan it here, pick the Ardia Sync folder in iCloud Drive, and check that tabs show up on both.

Known limit: Ardia can't be set as the default browser yet.

Send feedback with a screenshot from TestFlight, or shake the phone while the app is open.
