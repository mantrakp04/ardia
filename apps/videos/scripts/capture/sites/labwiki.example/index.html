Safety

Last edited Oct 31, 2026 by Ingrid HaldenReviewed yearlyv14

Required reading for everyone who works in rooms 2.14 to 2.18, including visiting students. Read it, sign the acknowledgement on the right, then ask Oskar for your hood and freezer induction.

Never work alone with phenol or chloroform. Both are used in DNA extraction. Use them only in a fume hood, with a second person in the room, nitrile over-gloves and the sash below the arrow.

Fume hoods

  1. Check the airflow monitor reads green before you open the sash.
  2. Work at least 15 cm inside the hood, sash at or below the arrow.
  3. Log every solvent session in Hood 2.16-A log.
  4. Report any alarm to the building desk, then to Ingrid.
