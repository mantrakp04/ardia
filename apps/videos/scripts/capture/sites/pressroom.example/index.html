Lumo is a habit tracker for people who have quit habit trackers. Instead of punishing a missed day, Lumo counts the days you showed up and helps you start again tomorrow. Lumo 2.0 adds habit stacks, a weekly review and gentle reminders that follow you across time zones. Built by a team of seven in Port Alder, Lumo is free to start, with Lumo Plus at $3 a month. It launches publicly on iPhone and Android on November 18, 2026.
