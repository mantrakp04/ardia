Lab 4: Enzymes

How temperature changes the rate of catalase, the enzyme that splits hydrogen peroxide.

2 h 50 minPairsPre-lab due Oct 6, 9:00Goggles and gloves

By the end of this lab you can

1

Measure an enzyme's reaction rate from the oxygen it releases.

2

Find catalase's optimum temperature and explain why activity falls above it.

Procedure 7 steps · step 4 of 7Print steps

✓

Prepare the extract

Blend 25 g potato in 50 mL cold buffer, filter through cheesecloth and keep the extract on ice.

✓

Set up five water baths 10 min

Bring baths to 4, 22, 37, 55 and 70 °C. Check each with a thermometer, not the dial.

✓

Equilibrate the tubes 5 min

Put 2 mL extract in each labelled tube and let it sit in its bath.

4

Start the reaction Timed · 60 s

Add 2 mL of 3% H₂O₂, seal the tube with the gas syringe and start the timer. Read the O₂ volume every 10 s.
