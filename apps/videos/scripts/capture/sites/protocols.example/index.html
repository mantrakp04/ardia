PCR Protocol v3 · ITS amplification from ectomycorrhizal root tips

Amplifies the fungal ITS region from single root tips for Sanger or amplicon sequencing. v3 adds a 10% overage to the master mix and drops the annealing step to 55 °C after the October failures.
