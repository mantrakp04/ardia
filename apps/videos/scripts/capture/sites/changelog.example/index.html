Lumo 2.0: habits that come in a chain

The biggest update since launch. Stack habits after a cue you already have, look back on your week every Sunday, and stop losing streaks to time zones.

NEWWhat's new

  • Habit stacks. Chain habits after a cue (“after coffee”, “when I get home”) and check them off in order. Stacks have their own streak.
  • Weekly review. Every Sunday evening, a one-screen look back: days you showed up, your best habit, and one to make smaller.
  • Home screen widget for today's habits, with check-off right from the widget on iPhone.

IMPROVEDBetter

  • Reminders follow your time zone. Fly from Port Alder to anywhere and your 7:30 nudge arrives at 7:30 local.
  • Onboarding is down to four screens and starts with picking three habits.

FIXEDFixed

  • Streaks no longer reset at midnight on Daylight Saving day. Sorry, everyone in March.
  • Exported CSV now includes notes. Field note was empty since 1.8.
